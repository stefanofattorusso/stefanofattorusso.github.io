Privacy Policy

Parsimonia is built around the Kakebo philosophy of mindful, intentional spending. That same philosophy extends to data: we collect only what is strictly necessary to improve app stability, and never what is personal to you. Your financial journal remains entirely private, local-first, and under your absolute control.

01 About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Parsimonia ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use our Android application. It applies to all users worldwide, regardless of country of residence.

By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this document. If you disagree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the App.

02 Local-First Architecture & Data We Do Not Collect

Parsimonia is designed from the ground up as a local-first, account-free application. We operate no proprietary user database or central servers holding your personal or financial records. We do not collect, transmit, or store on our servers any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or login credentials
  • Your financial entries, budgets, income figures, notes, or spending reflections
  • Your bank account details, payment card information, or credentials
  • Your device's precise or approximate location
  • Photos, videos, or files from your device storage
  • Any information that directly identifies you as an individual

All budgeting information you record resides strictly in a local on-device database on your phone and is never sent to us.

03 Optional Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Synchronization

Parsimonia includes an optional feature that allows you to synchronize your household budget with a partner or across your own secondary Android device. Because we do not run user accounts or cloud databases, this sync works on a decentralized, peer-to-peer (P2P) model built on the following privacy and security guarantees:

Zero-Knowledge End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)

Before any data leaves your phone, your recent active budget data (the current and preceding month) is compressed and encrypted on your device using authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption with keys derived via HKDF-SHA256 from a unique master pairing secret. Only you and the device you personally pair hold this key.

Hardware-Backed Secret Storage

Your sync master secret is wrapped and protected inside the hardware-backed Android Keystore. Sensitive keys are never transmitted unencrypted and are scrubbed from device memory when not in use.

Decentralized Relays & Traffic-Analysis Protection

Sync payloads are passed between paired devices as encrypted, ephemeral dead-drop events over decentralized public Nostr relays (using protocol NIP-78). Relays receive only authentic BIP-340 Schnorr cryptographic signatures and sealed ciphertext. Furthermore, sync payloads are padded to fixed 4 KB block boundaries before encryption, preventing relay operators and network observers from inferring transaction counts or activity patterns.

Tombstone Privacy & Data Deletion

When you delete an entry, the deletion propagates to your paired partner as a soft-delete tombstone. To ensure privacy, any textual notes or emotional reflections associated with that entry are permanently wiped from the tombstone record before it is synchronized.

You can view, edit, or reset the relay servers in the App settings at any time. Unpairing your device immediately purges the cryptographic keys from your device's Keystore, wipes sync preferences, and ceases all synchronization activity.

04 Device Permissions & Local Features

The App requests only the device permissions strictly necessary to deliver its core features:

  • Camera Access (Optional): The App requests camera access solely when you choose to pair a device by scanning your partner's pairing QR code. The camera stream is analyzed entirely in real-time on-device using ML Kit / CameraX to decode the pairing string. No images, video frames, or photos are ever recorded, saved, or uploaded. If you prefer not to grant camera access, you can pair devices manually by copying and pasting the pairing code string.
  • Notifications (Optional): The App uses the Android notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) to deliver daily mindful reflection reminders scheduled locally using Android's WorkManager. These reminders are generated and triggered 100% on-device; no external push messaging server (such as Firebase Cloud Messaging) is used.
  • Local Backup & Restore: You may export or import a full backup of your budgeting ledger via the Android system file picker (Storage Access Framework). The backup file is saved directly to your chosen local folder or personal storage. We have no access to these backup files and never upload them to any server.

05 Data Processed by Third-Party Services

To maintain stability, diagnose crashes, and safely manage feature rollouts, the App integrates selected Google Firebase SDKs. These services process limited, anonymized technical telemetry:

Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage patterns — such as which screens are viewed most frequently and how often features are used. This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally. Google may collect anonymized device identifiers and app interaction events.

You may opt out of analytics data collection via your device's advertising settings or system privacy controls.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Firebase Crashlytics & Performance Monitoring

We use Firebase Crashlytics to automatically detect, diagnose, and resolve application crashes, and Firebase Performance Monitoring to measure startup times. These reports contain stack traces, device model, operating system version, and anonymized diagnostic identifiers. They never contain your financial entries, amounts, or personal notes.

Firebase Privacy Overview: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Firebase Remote Config

We use Firebase Remote Config to fetch dynamic application configuration parameters and feature flags (such as gating experimental features or managing sync rollout). It retrieves non-personal configuration flags and evaluates anonymous criteria (e.g., app version, OS level). It does not collect or transmit personal financial data.

Firebase Privacy Overview: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Decentralized Nostr Relay Network (When Sync is Enabled)

If and only if you activate peer-to-peer sync, the App connects via encrypted WebSockets (wss://) to public Nostr relays. As with any internet connection, relay servers see your IP address during active socket transmission. However, because all sync payloads are end-to-end encrypted and padded before leaving your device, relay operators cannot read your financial records or associate them with your real-world identity.

06 Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable under data protection laws (such as the GDPR in the European Economic Area):

  • Legitimate Interest: Processing aggregated analytics, crash logs, and remote configuration flags is based on our legitimate interest in maintaining application stability, security, and performance.
  • User Consent & Action: Optional device capabilities (camera access for QR pairing, notification prompts, and peer-to-peer sync) are activated exclusively upon your explicit action or permission grant in the App.

No personal data is sold, rented, or monetized, and no profiling or targeted behavioral advertising is performed.

07 Your Rights & Control

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have statutory rights concerning data processed about you:

  • Right to access and portability: You can export all your local financial entries at any time using the built-in Backup & Export tool.
  • Right to erasure (deletion): Because all budgeting records are stored on your device only, you can permanently delete all your data at any time by clearing the App's data in Android Settings or uninstalling the App. Unpairing a synced device permanently destroys the local cryptographic keys.
  • Right to object or opt-out: You can revoke camera or notification permissions at any time in your Android system settings, and disable analytics tracking via device-level privacy settings.

For requests regarding technical telemetry retained by Google Firebase, inquiries may be directed through Google's privacy tools or by contacting us.

08 Data Retention & Security

Your financial ledger is retained locally on your device until you choose to delete entries, restore a backup, clear app storage, or uninstall the App. We have no remote access to your device and cannot retrieve or wipe your local records on your behalf.

Telemetry and crash reports collected by Firebase are retained according to Google's standard data retention schedules (typically up to 90 days for crash logs and 14 months for analytics). All network communications (Firebase telemetry and encrypted Nostr relay events) use TLS / WSS encryption in transit.
